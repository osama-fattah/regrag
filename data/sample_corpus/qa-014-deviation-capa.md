# QA-014 Deviation Management and CAPA

Northwind Biologics (fictional company). Synthetic SOP written for the RegRAG demo corpus.

## Scope

This SOP applies to unplanned departures from approved procedures, specifications or batch records at the Cork manufacturing site and its contract manufacturers.

## Reporting a deviation

Any employee who observes a deviation must record it in the electronic quality management system (eQMS) by the end of the same shift. The area supervisor must acknowledge the record within one business day.

## Classification

Deviations are classified as minor, major or critical. A minor deviation has no plausible impact on product quality or patient safety. A major deviation could affect product quality but is unlikely to harm patients. A critical deviation has a reasonable likelihood of harming patients or breaches a marketing authorisation commitment. Critical deviations must be escalated to the Qualified Person and the Head of Quality within 24 hours.

## Investigation

Root cause investigations must be completed within 30 calendar days of the deviation being recorded. Investigators should use a structured method such as the five whys or a fishbone (Ishikawa) diagram. An extension beyond 30 days requires written approval from Quality Assurance with a justification and a new due date.

## Corrective and preventive actions (CAPA)

Each major or critical deviation must have at least one CAPA. A CAPA effectiveness check is performed 90 days after the CAPA is implemented, using objective evidence such as a reduced recurrence rate. A CAPA that fails its effectiveness check is reopened and the root cause is re-examined.

## Batch disposition

A batch affected by an open major or critical deviation cannot be released until the Qualified Person has reviewed the investigation outcome.
