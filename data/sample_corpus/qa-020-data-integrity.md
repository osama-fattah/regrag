# QA-020 Data Integrity Policy

Northwind Biologics (fictional company). Synthetic policy written for the RegRAG demo corpus.

## Principles

All GxP records must meet the ALCOA+ principles: attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original and accurate, plus complete, consistent, enduring and available. These principles apply equally to paper records, electronic records and hybrid systems.

## User accounts

Shared or generic user accounts are prohibited on GxP computerised systems. Each user must have a unique account so that every action is attributable to one person. Passwords must not be shared, and accounts of leavers must be disabled within one business day of their departure.

## Audit trails

Audit trails must be enabled on every GxP computerised system and must capture who made a change, what was changed, when, and why. System owners must review audit trails for critical data at least monthly, and before batch release for data used in release decisions. Audit trails must not be editable or deletable by ordinary users.

## Record retention

GxP records are retained for at least the period required by the applicable regulation and never less than ten years after the expiry of the batch they relate to. Electronic records must remain readable for the full retention period, including after system migrations.

## Reporting data integrity concerns

Employees who suspect data falsification or manipulation must report it to Quality Assurance or through the confidential ethics line. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited.
