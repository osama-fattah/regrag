# HR-010 GxP Training

Northwind Biologics (fictional company). Synthetic SOP written for the RegRAG demo corpus.

## Training requirement

Every employee and contractor who performs GxP activities must be trained on the procedures relevant to their role before performing those activities unsupervised.

## Curricula

Each role has a training curriculum maintained in the learning management system (LMS). Line managers review their team members' curricula at least once a year and whenever a role changes.

## New and revised procedures

When a procedure is revised, affected staff must complete training on the new version before its effective date. The default period between approval and effective date is 14 calendar days, which gives staff time to complete training.

## Training effectiveness

Training on critical procedures, including aseptic processing and ICSR handling, includes an assessment. A score of at least 80% is required to pass. Staff who fail may retake the assessment once before retraining by a subject matter expert is required.

## Overdue training

Staff with overdue training on a procedure must not perform the related activity. Training overdue by more than 30 days is reported to the site Quality Council each month.
