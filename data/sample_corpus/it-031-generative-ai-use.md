# IT-031 Acceptable Use of Generative AI

Northwind Biologics (fictional company). Synthetic policy written for the RegRAG demo corpus.

## Approved tools

Employees may only use generative AI tools listed in the Northwind AI tool register. Public consumer chatbots are not approved for any business use. New tools are added to the register after a security, privacy and legal review.

## Prohibited inputs

Patient-identifiable data, unpublished clinical trial results, trade secrets and personal data of employees must never be entered into a generative AI tool unless the tool is approved for that data classification in the register.

## Human oversight

Generative AI output used in a GxP context, a regulatory submission or a communication to a health authority must be reviewed and approved by a qualified person before use. The reviewer is accountable for the content, not the tool.

## Transparency and logging

Internal assistants built by Northwind must show the sources used for each answer and must log each request with the user, the time, the retrieved documents and the model version. Logs are kept for at least two years to support audits and incident investigations.

## AI inventory and risk classification

Every AI system in use is recorded in the AI inventory with an owner, intended purpose, risk classification and the date of its last review. Systems that could be high-risk under the EU AI Act are referred to the AI Governance Board before deployment.

## Incidents

Suspected harmful, biased or incorrect AI output that has been used in a decision must be reported to the AI Governance Board within five business days.
