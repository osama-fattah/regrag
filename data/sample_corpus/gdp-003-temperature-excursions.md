# GDP-003 Management of Temperature Excursions

Northwind Biologics (fictional company). Synthetic SOP written for the RegRAG demo corpus.

## Storage conditions

Biologic finished products must be stored and transported at 2 °C to 8 °C unless the product label states otherwise. Warehouses and vehicles are continuously monitored by calibrated data loggers that record temperature at least every five minutes.

## Definition of an excursion

A temperature excursion is any recorded temperature outside the labelled storage range, regardless of duration. Excursions shorter than 15 minutes that stay between 0 °C and 12 °C are classed as minor; all others are classed as significant.

## Immediate actions

When an excursion is detected, the affected stock is placed in quarantine and moved back to the correct storage condition. Quarantined stock must not be shipped or sold until Quality has made a disposition decision.

## Assessment

Quality reviews the logger data, the product's stability data and the cumulative time out of refrigeration for the batch. Significant excursions require a deviation record under QA-014. The disposition decision is made by the Qualified Person within five business days.

## Frozen product

Any biologic product exposed to a temperature below 0 °C must be rejected, because freezing can irreversibly damage the protein structure, unless stability data specifically supports freezing.
