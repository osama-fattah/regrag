# PV-001 Individual Case Safety Report (ICSR) Handling

Northwind Biologics (fictional company). Synthetic SOP written for the RegRAG demo corpus.

## Purpose

This procedure describes how the Pharmacovigilance (PV) department receives, assesses and submits individual case safety reports for marketed products in the European Economic Area.

## Day zero

Day zero is the date on which any Northwind employee or contracted partner first becomes aware of a report that meets the four minimum criteria. The clock starts on day zero even if the information reaches a sales representative, a call-centre agent or a medical science liaison rather than the PV team. Employees must forward safety information to the PV inbox within one business day.

## Minimum criteria for a valid case

A case is valid only when it contains all four minimum criteria: at least one identifiable reporter, at least one identifiable patient, at least one suspected adverse reaction, and at least one suspect medicinal product. Cases missing any criterion are logged as incomplete and the PV associate starts follow-up to obtain the missing information.

## Submission timelines

Valid serious cases must be submitted to EudraVigilance within 15 calendar days of day zero. Valid non-serious cases from the EEA must be submitted within 90 calendar days of day zero. Follow-up information that is significant restarts the clock for that follow-up report.

## Seriousness assessment

A reaction is serious if it results in death, is life-threatening, requires or prolongs hospitalisation, results in persistent or significant disability, is a congenital anomaly, or is judged medically important by the PV physician. When the reporter and the PV physician disagree, the case is processed using the more conservative assessment.

## Quality checks

Every serious case undergoes a second-person quality review before submission. The PV quality team samples 10% of non-serious cases each month and reports error rates to the PV Lead.
