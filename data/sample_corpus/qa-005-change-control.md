# QA-005 Change Control

Northwind Biologics (fictional company). Synthetic SOP written for the RegRAG demo corpus.

## When change control applies

Change control applies to any planned change to validated equipment, computerised systems, processes, materials, suppliers, specifications or facilities that could affect product quality, patient safety or regulatory compliance.

## Raising a change request

The change owner raises a change request in the eQMS describing the current state, the proposed change, the justification and the affected products. No change may be implemented before the change request is approved.

## Impact assessment

Subject matter experts from Quality, Regulatory Affairs, Validation and Manufacturing assess the impact of the change. Regulatory Affairs determines whether the change requires a variation to the marketing authorisation, and if so whether it is a Type IA, Type IB or Type II variation.

## Approval and implementation

Changes are approved by Quality Assurance. Changes that require a Type II variation cannot be implemented for commercial supply until the health authority has approved the variation. Emergency changes may be implemented with verbal QA approval but must be documented in the eQMS within two business days.

## Closure

A change request is closed only after all actions are complete and the effectiveness of the change has been verified. Change requests open for more than 180 days are escalated to the site Quality Council.
